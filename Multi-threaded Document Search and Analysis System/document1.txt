The Internet of Things, or IoT, connects everyday objects to the internet.
Smart homes use IoT devices such as thermostats, door locks and lights.
A smart thermostat learns when people are home and saves energy.
Many iot devices send small amounts of data many times a day.
Security is a big concern because cheap IOT devices often ship with default passwords.

Updating firmware regularly keeps devices safer.
In Kigali, IoT sensors can help monitor traffic, and IoT cameras can report accidents.
Good network design matters more as the number of connected devices grows.
