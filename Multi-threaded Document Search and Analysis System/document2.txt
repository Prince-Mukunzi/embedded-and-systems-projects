Agriculture is one of the fastest growing areas for IoT.
Soil moisture sensors tell farmers exactly when to water their crops.
Weather stations send readings to a phone app every ten minutes.
An iot gateway collects data from sensors spread across a large farm.
Batteries in field sensors can last for years with low power radios.
Farmers compare IoT data with past harvests, and the IoT dashboard shows trends.
Drones take photos of crops to spot disease early.
Cooperatives can share one gateway to keep costs low.
Clean data is the first step to good decisions.
The IOT market for farming keeps growing every year.
