Hospitals use connected devices to watch patients around the clock.
Wearable heart monitors send alerts to nurses when readings look unusual.
Medical IoT must follow strict privacy rules.
Patient data should be encrypted before it leaves the device.
Some clinics track medicine fridges with IoT temperature sensors.
If a fridge gets too warm, an alarm sends a text message to staff.
Remote care helps people in rural areas see a doctor without travelling.
The future of iot in health care depends on trust, and trust in IoT depends on security.
